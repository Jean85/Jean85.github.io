In the upcoming Symfony 8.2 release, there will be a new transport for Messenger, based on MongoDB, as announced in https://symfony.com/blog/new-in-symfony-8-2-more-mongodb-integrations#mongodb-messenger-transport.

I personally contributed this feature, but in reality it's not written from scratch: instead, Symfony adopted it from my open source project [facile-it/mongodb-messenger-transport](https://github.com/facile-it/mongodb-messenger-transport). It got slightly transformed in the process, but I'm happy to see that that work led to this, and it will benefit from further contributions inside the whole Symfony ecosystem.
